Buggy Blast

Buggy Blast is an action-packed off-road racer featuring 12 World Circuit stages. Start with one track and finish first to unlock the next challenge. Race four laps through forests, mountain nights, snow, storms, desert and coastal tracks, then master six reverse routes. Time your nitro bursts, drift through corners and launch off ramps to beat three fierce rivals. Collect coins, unlock four powerful buggies and upgrade your favorite ride. Every race ends with a live classification while your buggy continues on autopilot.
