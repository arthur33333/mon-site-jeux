Chancla Fury Auntie Bertha World

The superheroes thought they were unstoppable. Then they met Auntie Bertha! Chancla Fury is a hilarious classic arcade-style fighting game. A tough granny takes on gym bros, caped heroes, disco dancers, ninjas, sumo twins, giant robots and a cosmic grandmaster, armed with her flip-flops, her handbag and a kick so stinky it knocks heroes dizzy. Slap combos, throw boomerang flip-flops, clash energy balls, grab power-ups and unleash the ULTRA CHANCLA BEAM! 3 game modes: - Story Mode: fight through 21 levels and beat every hero in town - Survival Mode: endless waves of fighters with only 1 life - Versus Mode: 1 player vs the computer, or 2 players on one keyboard Pick Easy, Normal or Hard, unlock new fighters with their own voices and super powers, collect stars and earn 12 achievements.
