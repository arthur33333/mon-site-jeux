Snow Plow Inc.

Snow Plow Inc. is a relaxing snow plowing game. Drive your orange plow truck through deep snow and thick ice, fill your truck bed with snow blocks, then head back to the yard and sell your load for coins. LEVELS: 15 hand-built levels. The snow gets tougher as you go, from soft powder to packed snow and solid ice. Clear the field to earn up to 3 stars and unlock the next level. UPGRADES: Spend coins on plow count, plow teeth, plow power, engine, earnings and capacity. Visit the Shop for fuel and boosters, and log in daily for rewards. Progress saves automatically.
