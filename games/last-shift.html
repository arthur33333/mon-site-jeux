Last Shift

Last Shift: Humans vs Machines is a fast 3D top-down action game. Technology is slowly taking over and five ordinary workers fight back. CHARACTERS: Programmer (homing code bolts), Soldier (rapid-fire rifle), Financier (bouncing coins, extra gold), Cleaner (slowing soap spray) and Garbage Collector (heavy piercing trash bags). LEVELS: 5 chapters with 10 rooms each - Smart City (robots and drones), Server Farm, Global Warming, The Parliament (devil-horned politicians) and The Singularity. Every chapter ends with a boss: The Supercomputer, AI Overlord, Carbon Colossus, The Chancellor and The Singularity. Dodge enemy fire, stand still to attack automatically and pick a new ability on every level-up: Twin Shot, Ricochet, Chain Lightning, Orbital Strike, Plasma Orbs, Force Field and more. MODES: Campaign, Survival, Crypto Heist, Boss Rush, Skyscraper (endless floors) and Server Maze. Upgrade gear, unlock heroes and talents, and complete daily quests.
