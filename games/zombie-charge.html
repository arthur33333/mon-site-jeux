Zombie Charge

Zombie Charge is a fast lane-runner shooter. Your hero sprints down the street and fires automatically while hordes of zombies block the road. Steer left and right through the gates: good ones add soldiers, multiply your squad or boost damage and fire rate, bad ones weaken you, and a few shots can power a gate up. Boss levels put a huge zombie in your way, so arrive with a squad big enough to bring it down. Features: - Level after level across four worlds: city, desert, snow and neon night - Boss fights with big health bars - 5 weapons to unlock and upgrade: pistol, SMG, shotgun, minigun and laser - 6 heroes, each with a perk: extra soldiers, bonus coins, more health, damage or fire rate - Coins after every level and a lucky wheel that can multiply them - Upgrade damage, fire rate and health between runs - Plays in 8 languages on desktop and mobile Pick your gates wisely, grow your squad and charge through the undead!
