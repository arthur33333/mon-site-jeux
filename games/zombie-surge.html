Zombie Surge

A zombie horde is marching on your fence, and carts full of weapons roll along the tracks beside it. Every bullet is a choice: shoot the crowd, or smash a cart for a bigger gun. Go for the cart and the zombies get closer. Ignore it and you are stuck with a pistol. Six guns from pistol to machine gun, fire-rate and damage buffs, bomb carts and combo streaks. Seven locations: desert, graveyard, city, forest, winter, Japanese temple and seaport. Each one ends with a boss with its own trick: the Bulldozer charges down a red lane, the Necromancer raises the dead, the Ironclad hides behind armor plates. Levels of three waves each, an endless Horde mode, daily rewards and missions. Spend the gold from every run on upgrades: damage, fire rate, fence strength and starting weapon. Progress is saved in the browser.
