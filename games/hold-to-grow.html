Hold To Grow

Hold to Grow is a one-button 3D runner. Hold to grow bigger, release to shrink! Run along a wooden bridge over the sea and change your size to get past every obstacle: grow big to smash brick walls and wooden crates, stay small to squeeze through narrow gates and duck under bars. LEVELS: 25 handcrafted levels with 10 obstacle types (walls, gates, giants, cracks, stone blocks, coin gates and more), then endless levels that keep getting harder. MODE: At the end of every level a giant boss waits. Hold to charge the power meter and release in the gold zone to punch him down the multiplier runway and win up to x10 coins. CHARACTERS: Unlock 12 h
