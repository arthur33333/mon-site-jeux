Unbolt Run

Unbolt Run combines 3D running with fast-paced bolt puzzles. Follow the arrows and remove bolts in the correct order to lift steel gates before your escape margin runs out. Solid bulkhead gates hide a final latch behind support bolts, adding another challenge. Complete the six-gate First Escape mission, then tackle Endless mode. Solve increasingly tricky puzzles, explore changing tunnel colors, and beat your personal best. Simple click or tap controls make it easy to start, while quick thinking keeps your run alive.
