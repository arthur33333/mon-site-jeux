Million Coin Vault

Sneak into the bank and take EVERYTHING. Million Coin Vault is a cozy first-person coin-scooping game where every coin in the pile is a real coin, and the pile visibly shrinks as you take it. Start with your bare hands in a little corner-bank safe holding 10,000 coins. Fill your bag, carry it to the deposit machine and watch the counter roll. Spend your loot on bigger bags, a shovel, a coin magnet and a mini excavator. Learn skills, set down a Pocket Bank right next to the pile, and build Coin Chutes that keep working while youre away. Empty a vault and the great round door swings open to a bigger one: the Corner Bank Safe, the marble Town Bank Vault, the Grand Vault with 1,000,000 coins, and finally the Dragons Hoard: 100,000,000 coins guarded by a sleeping dragon. Make too much noise and it wakes up!
