COLD IMPACT: Rocket Missions

COLD IMPACT: Rocket Missions is a fast one-more-run 3D arcade game. Charge your rocket, launch it and smash through a destructible world: tanks, helicopters, bridges, then giant Titans and an abyssal sea boss. 2 levels: a ground-to-air campaign with a boss at the end, then an amphibious level where you dive and fly again. Between flights, earn coins and upgrade your rocket (wings, reactor, turbo), your launcher and your cargo to fly further every run. Open the chest after a few flights for a big reward. Single player. Made with AI tools (code by Claude Code, some art by Gemini).
