Ring Unhook

Ring Unhook is a calm rotating-ring puzzle. Colorful rings are hooked onto each other, and every ring has a gap. HOW TO PLAY: drag a ring to spin it around its center. Turn its gap to the hook that holds it and the ring slips free. Some rings are locked, chained or threaded on bars, so work out the right order. Free every ring to clear the level. LEVELS: 100 levels in 4 chapters that start simple and grow into tricky tangles of hooks, locks and chains. Beat the move target to earn 3 stars. TOOLS: stuck? Use Undo, Restart, Hint, the Hammer to break a hook, or the Rocket to blast the most tangled ring. MODES: play the main levels, race the clock or a move limit in Challenge mode, and solve a new Daily puzzle every day. Fill the chest bar and build your win streak. Relaxing, satisfying, and your progress is saved automatically.
