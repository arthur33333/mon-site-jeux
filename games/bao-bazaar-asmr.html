Bao Bazaar ASMR

Bao Bazaar ASMR is a cozy trading and collecting game about cute squishy bao buns. OPEN PACKS: pick a pack from the shelf, swipe to tear it open and lift the bamboo steamer lid to reveal a new bun of R, SR, SSR or UR rarity. SQUISH: tap a bun for a relaxing close-up, then poke, drag and squish it with soft ASMR sounds. TRADE 1-ON-1: sit down with bot traders in a turn-based deal. Offer buns or cash, ask for more, then accept or deny before the timer runs out while the Good Trade / Bad Trade meter shows who wins. MARKET MODE: bun prices move through the day. Read the price chart, buy low and sell high. CHARACTERS: over 100 bao buns to collect, from Strawberry Shortcake and Foxy to the Wizard and the shining Sun bun. Fill themed collection rooms, upgrade your buns and claim daily rewards and quests. LEVELS: your level grows with every pack you open, with no level cap. All coins and trades are virtual. Progress is saved automatically.
