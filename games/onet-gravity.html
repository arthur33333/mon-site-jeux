Onet Gravity

Onet Gravity is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle with a twist: gravity! Tap two identical tiles to connect them with a line of up to 3 straight segments (2 turns) - the line may even go around the outside of the board. Matched pairs disappear and the tiles above fall down, opening new paths and blocking others, so the order of your moves really matters. Earn a star for every pair you connect. Every level brings a new tile set - fruits, cute pets, bags &amp; hats or flowers - with its own background and music. A short tutorial shows the rules, then the boards grow from small warm-ups to big 7x9 master puzzles. No timer: think ahead, use the free u
