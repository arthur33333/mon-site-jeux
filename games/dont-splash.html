DONT SPLASH

Dont Splash is a 3D horror platformer set in an endless abandoned pool. Jump from tile to tile through the fog and find the exit, but never fall in: a blind creature lives in the water and hunts by sound. One splash and it comes straight for you! Cross nine rooms of sinking tiles, rotating bridges, drifting floors and crushing walls, throw pebbles and bottles to lure the monster away, and photograph the hidden code to unlock the final door. Play Solo, or team up in 2 Player split-screen on one keyboard: revive your partner and escape together!
