Holdem Royale

Holdem Royale is Texas Holdem poker with real players, live. Pull up a chair in a Wild West saloon, buy in, and outplay the table. Every player at the table buys in for the same amount, so everyone starts even. Quiet tables are topped up with house players, so theres always a game. Pick your stakes: tables from 200 to 2,500 chips, and High Stakes tables from 5,000 to 40,000 once your stack is big enough. Want to practice first? Solo tables pit you against five house players with practice chips that never touch your balance.
