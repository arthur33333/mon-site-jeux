Droppy World

Droppy World is a colorful endless platformer starring Droppy, a cheerful little drop. Run, jump and dodge through three hand-drawn worlds: Green World, Lava World and Space. Beat three giant bosses - a Stone Golem, a fiery Phoenix and an Alien Mothership - each with its own animated attacks. Modes: Easy, Medium, Hard and Legend. Collect coins to unlock 9 skins, 7 companions and 10 powers. Stomp enemies, chain combos and beat your record! In English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and German.
