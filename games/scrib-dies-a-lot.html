Scrib Dies a Lot

Scrib Dies a Lot is an inventive 2D paper-folding puzzle platformer set entirely inside a students sketchbook. Guide Scrib, a fragile sketched hero, through hazardous hand-drawn notebook pages packed with pencil tips, coffee stains, paperclips, staples, and wet ink spills. When the path forward seems impossible, fold and crease the page itself to connect distant platforms, bridge bottomless tears, and bypass deadly obstacles to reach the exit portal! Featuring 100 handcrafted levels, clever spatial puzzles, and multiple unique worlds.
