Chubby Finn

**ChubbyFinn** is a cute Sokoban-style puzzle game where you help an adorable chubby seal push balls into the correct target positions. Move Finn in four directions, plan every step carefully, and remember: balls can be pushed but never pulled! Each level challenges your logic, strategy, and problem-solving skills with tricky layouts. Solve every puzzle, avoid getting stuck, and help ChubbyFinn become the ultimate puzzle master!
