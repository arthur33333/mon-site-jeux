Villadash Hero Rescue

Morvane and his bandits are raiding the villages of the kingdom, and every villager he carries off makes him stronger. You have one round to get to them first. Pick Ronan the knight, Lyra the rogue or Brann the frost druid and race through the village against the clock. Knock out the guards around each group of captives, hold to cut them free and send them running for the gate, while Morvane walks to the nearest group to drag it away. Every group you save is a point for you and more strength for the duel against him at the end of the round. The campaign has 7 villages, each with a new rule: archers across the river, villagers locked inside houses, recruits who fight at your side, houses on fire, a night convoy to the bonfires, and a final battle at Morvanes citadel where you switch between all three heroes. Heroes level up from 1 to 10 for stronger abilities. Earn stars, spend coins on training and outfits, and climb the local top 10 of each village.
