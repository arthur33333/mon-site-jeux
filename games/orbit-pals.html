Orbit Pals

Orbit Pals is a cozy space sorting puzzle with adorable alien pals. Tap a planet to lift the pals at its edge, then tap another planet to make them hop over. Gather every species into its own flock - each planet fits only four! Levels: endless generated levels with 3-star ratings, a tutorial, and home-planet levels from level 15. Modes: Daily Puzzle with streaks and Star Pals rewards. Characters: 9 kinds of pals and 81 hybrids to create in the Lab. Plus a Home Planet that earns coins while youre away, a Cosmic Wheel and planet styles. 7 languages, desktop and mobile.
