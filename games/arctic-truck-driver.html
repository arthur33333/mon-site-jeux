Arctic Truck Driver

Arctic Truck Driver is a realistic 3D ice road trucking game. Haul cargo across frozen lakes where the ice decides how fast you can go. Thick ice is safe, but dark ice with glowing cracks is thin &ndash; drive too fast and it breaks under your truck. Choose jobs from the job board: mining supplies, a fuel tanker that sloshes when you brake, long pipe bundles, sensitive explosives, or rescue missions where you tow a stranded pickup to camp. Drive in convoys and keep your distance, steer around open water, slow down for pressure ridges and watch the ice stress meter.
