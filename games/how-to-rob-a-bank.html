How to Rob a Bank

How to Rob a Bank is a 3D heist game inspired by the 2026 movie How to Rob a Bank, where a crew in animal masks livestreams a bank robbery while the police close in. You play the wolf, across three stages. The Breach: clear the banking hall, shoot out the cameras, crack three vault terminals. The Stream: hold the lobby through four police waves while SWAT fast rope from the ceiling. The Getaway: outrun the police cars for 3200 metres, drifting every corner. Style raises the hype meter, which drops armour, health and bullet time. Crew: Wolf, Bear, Rabbit, Raccoon.
