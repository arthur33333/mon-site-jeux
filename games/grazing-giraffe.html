Grazing Giraffe

Grazing Giraffe is a cozy sliding puzzle game about one very hungry, very long giraffe. Swipe to send your giraffe gliding across the savanna. It keeps sliding until it hits something, stretching its neck behind it as it goes. Fill every tile of the trail to eat all the leaves and finish the level. It sounds simple, but you can only stop where a wall, tree or pond stops you, and your own neck gets in the way. Plan each move, find the one path that fills the whole board, and use rewind whenever you paint yourself into a corner.
