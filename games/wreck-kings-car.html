Wreck Kings Car

Wreck Kings: Car Destruction Island is a 3D racing and demolition derby game by BREYMANTECH. Smash, crash and outlast your rivals on an action-packed island! Play 300 career events across 15 chapters, with a boss at the end of each chapter. There are 8 modes: Circuit Race, Sprint, Dirt Rally, Demolition Derby, Stunt Show, Smash Hunt, Takedown and Offroad Rush. Earn cash to buy 4 vehicles: a hot hatch, a supercar, a monster truck and a heavy truck. Upgrade the engine, nitro, tires, armor and suspension, and unlock 18 skills in the skill tree. Car bodies dent and parts fly off when you crash. Design your own livery in the Paint Shop
