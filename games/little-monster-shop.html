Little Monster Shop

Run a tiny market for cute monsters! Harvest glowing mushrooms, stock the shelves and serve a line of hungry customers at the till. Spend your coins to unlock new fields, shelves and staff, then open wilder markets. Game mode: single player shop management. Levels: three markets with their own products and monsters: Mushroom Garden, Ice Cave and Lava Bazaar. Ice floors make you slide and products melt on the shelves in the heat. Characters: your blue shopkeeper, helpers, cashiers and collectors, plus special customers: giants who buy in bulk, crowned VIPs who pay triple and thieves you have to catch. Features: ring the Golden Bell for a Rush Hour with double income, finish all three markets for a Grand Opening, earn stars for bigger profits, daily rewards and earnings while you are away. Available in English, Turkish and Russian.
