Black Hole Swallow Master

Black Hole Swallow Master is an exciting and addictive casual arcade game where you control a powerful black hole and swallow everything in your path. Move around the environment, collect objects, grow bigger, and challenge yourself to consume as much as possible before time runs out. Start small and carefully move your black hole across the level to swallow objects and increase your size. As your black hole game adventure continues, you can consume larger objects and clear more of the environment. Plan your movement, find the best targets, and become the ultimate swallowing master.
