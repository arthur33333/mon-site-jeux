Siren City Ambulance Game

Every second counts in Siren City! You drive an ambulance on emergency calls across a busy 3D city. Switch on your siren, weave through traffic and get to the accident scene fast. Your paramedics jump out, carry the stretcher to the patient and load them into the ambulance. Then rush to the hospital, but drive smoothly: hard braking and crashes lower the patients Stability meter. Features: - 40 levels in 8 chapters: First Shift, Rush Hour, Night Calls, Market Mayhem, Highway Crash, Storm Emergencies, Midnight Pile-up and Disaster Day - Golden Minute timer: arrive early to earn bonus time for the hospital run - Stability meter: keep the ride
