Stellar Bastion

Stellar Bastion is an endless wave-survival shooter. You are the last gunner on a star-fortress flight deck, and the bugs only want one thing: you. Hold to move while your rifle auto-fires at the nearest bug &mdash; aiming is handled, so every decision is positioning. Waves escalate every 13 seconds, and every 5th wave a HIVE KING speeds up the whole swarm. Kills drop MGs, shotguns and rocket launchers with limited ammo; your MK-1 sidearm never runs dry. Level-ups offer 3-pick-1 perk cards from 22 upgrades without ever pausing the fight. How many waves will you bank?
