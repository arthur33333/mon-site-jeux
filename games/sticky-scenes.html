Sticky Scenes

Sticky Scenes is a cozy sticker puzzle game full of cute animals, snacks and treats. HOW IT WORKS: a board of stickers sits above a row of empty sticker shapes. Find the sticker that matches a shape and tap it to stick it in place. More shapes keep coming until the board is clear. THE BENCH: tapped a sticker that is not needed yet? It waits on the bench and jumps into its shape as soon as that shape comes up. The bench has only a few slots, and a full bench ends the level, so look carefully before you tap. BEAT THE CLOCK: every level has a timer. Stuck? Use a Hint to find the next sticker. LEVELS AND CHAPTERS: 47 levels in 11 chapters: a short tutorial, then 10 themed chapters: Pets, Farm, Underwater, Jungle Adventure, Ancient Egypt, Japan, Candy Kingdom, Halloween, Amusement Park, Wild West. STICKER BOOK: every win gives you new stickers. Stick them onto the chapter picture to complete each scene. Your progress is saved automatically.
