Blast Round Puzzle Block

Blast Round is a colorful block puzzle game where every move matters. Drag and place block pieces on the board, complete full rows and columns, build combos and earn points. Progress through challenging levels, face powerful bosses, charge your Mana Power and use special abilities to survive difficult situations. Hammer and Shuffle power-ups can help when the board gets crowded. The game features multiple visual themes, boss battles, progression, coins, upgrades and rewarding puzzle gameplay for desktop and mobile. Plan your moves carefully, create bigger combos and see how far you can go in Blast Round!
