One Last Clown

Davi, 9, jumped the fence of an abandoned school at night, chasing a red balloon. You go in after him with a flashlight that will not last until morning. The clowns walking the halls are the children who never left the school in 1994: Lia the doll, The Silent One who hunts by sound, and Pipoca, the giant clown who never stops looking. Each one follows its own rule. Read their notes, learn what they fear and hold the seal at their altar to set them free. Every child you free gives you a key. Three keys open the way out. Hide in lockers and hold your breath, throw cans to pull Pipoca away. Some safe rooms are not safe. The map changes every night, with a Daily Night mode and best times. 20 clues, a true ending and 6 languages. Use headphones: jump scares and loud sounds.
