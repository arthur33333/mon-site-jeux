Bridge Builder Cargo Transport

Bridge Builder Cargo Transport is a fun and challenging transport simulator where you take control of a utility truck loaded with bridge repair materials. A major bridge has been damaged, and only you can deliver the tools, steel, cement, and construction cargo needed to rebuild it. Drive through off-road paths, dusty routes, industrial areas, and tricky terrain while keeping your trailer balanced and your cargo safe. Every level brings new challenges, including narrow roads, sharp turns, unstable tracks, and delivery time pressure. Your goal is simple: transport the repair materials safely to the broken bridge.
