Duckling Rush.io

Duckling Rush.io is a cute pond arcade where you play a mama duck racing to gather the longest line of ducklings. Swim around a big pond, scoop up lost ducklings, and steal them from rival mamas by cutting through their line. Dodge the hungry pike, use your QUACK burst to speed ahead and make rivals wobble, and finish each 90 second round with the longest conga line to win bread. Every duckling has a personality: sleepy, brown, punk, rubber and rare golden ones worth extra.
