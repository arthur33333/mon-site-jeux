Deadpack

DEADPACK is a backpack management auto-battler where your loadout is your lifeline. Pack weapons into a limited inventory, merge matching gear into stronger versions, and fight hordes of monsters. Your hero attacks automatically, but you decide how to prepare for each battle. Combine swords, bows, shurikens, and magical weapons, then choose skills as you level up. Earn gold, expand your backpack, and upgrade your arsenal between adventures. Explore eight acts with 15 waves each, face powerful bosses, and adapt your strategy to new enemies.
