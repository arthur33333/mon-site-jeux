MISREGISTER

MISREGISTER: a four-page seam proof pressed on one cuboid. Ink lands on the page facing you, but after every shot the press turns to a page you did not pick and the ink keeps pressing down on all four. Three or more of one ink pop, even across pages, and anything left hanging falls. Drift adds a row to all four pages on a timer; one bubble over the trim rail spoils the edition. A pop scores 10, a fall 15, and 25 for each extra page one pop crossed. Drag to aim on desktop and phones.
