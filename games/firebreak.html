FireBreak

Take the controls of a firefighting aircraft and stop wildfires before they get out of hand. Scoop water from lakes and rivers, line up your run, and drop your load right on the flames. Fires spread fast, so choose your targets wisely and keep your tank full. Fly missions across six regions: desert canyon, alpine lake, coastal hills, Cinder Gulch, Ember Isle and Tallgrass Prairie, by day, at golden hour, at dusk and at night. Earn stars and credits, then unlock and upgrade new aircraft in the hangar, including a helitanker that can hover. Easy to pick up, hard to master. Plays great on desktop and mobile.
