Hush Maze: Horror Escape

Hush Maze is a scary maze escape in total darkness. You are a little kid in a yellow raincoat with only a brass bell. Ring it and an echo lights up the walls for a moment. Find the key, then the door. But the monster hears every ring! Hide in lockers, throw pebbles to lure it away, avoid creaky boards and puddles, pull loud levers to open gates. 60 levels in 3 haunted places: the Cellar (The Shade), the Garden (The Thorn) and the Clock Tower (The Ticker). Each place ends with a chase: follow the candles to the door! Ring less to earn 3 stars. Jump scares can be turned off in Settings.
