DRONE ZERO

Pilot DZ-01 and reclaim a city under alien siege! DRONE ZERO is a 3D arcade shooter packed with glowing gunfire, explosive battles, rain and lightning. Fight through eight missions, destroy enemy patrols, disable transmitters, intercept aerial attackers, defend an uplink and escort a convoy. Face powerful bosses using twin cannons, rockets, an EMP pulse and evasive dashes. Collect credits, restore your shields and upgrade your weapons, shields and engines in the hangar. Chain eliminations, earn mission stars and unlock trophies. Play with keyboard and mouse or touch controls.
