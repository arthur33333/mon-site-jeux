Bus Stop Hero

Take the wheel in Bus Bay Rush 3D, a city bus driving game. Pick up passengers waiting at the bus stops, pull into the BUS STOP bay and watch them climb aboard and take their seats. Then drive them across the city before the timer runs out. Follow the green GPS line and the minimap through busy streets full of traffic. When you reach a bay the bus parks itself, the camera swings round to the door and you can see your passengers sitting inside. Features: - 50 levels, from a quick two-stop run to rush-hour routes with six stops - 3 buses: City Blue, Red Express and Teal Comfort, each with its own speed, power and handling
