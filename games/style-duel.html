Style Duel

Style Duel is a dress up fashion battle. Pick a theme, then mix 75 pieces against the clock: 9 hairstyles, 12 dresses, 12 tops, 12 skirts and trousers, 12 pairs of shoes, 12 extras like tiaras, hats and handbags, and 6 makeup looks, most in 9 colours. Then walk the runway next to your rival while the audience votes with hearts! Story mode: 6 chapters and 6 rivals, up to the Royal Ball and Madame Lux. Contest mode: 10 themes, from Princess Day to Halloween Party and Sports Day. Follow the judges favourite styles, finish the whole look and keep your colours together to win the crown.
