Block Ship Battle

Raise the sails and fight for the richest hold on the reef! Block Ship Battle is a fast pirate brawler with real broadside combat: your cannons fire to the side, so every fight is a dance of turning, timing and reloading until your guns bear on the enemy &mdash; then KABOOM. Sink ships, scoop up floating treasure and carry your gold to a harbour &mdash; only banked gold counts, and a fat hold makes you the target everyone is hunting. Hunt the Treasure Galleon, sail into storms for the treasure they wash up, and level up during the match: every level fits your ship with a new upgrade you can see, up to your captains ultimate. Six ships and captains, each with two abilities and an ultimate. Five modes, from Plunder Brawl to Last Ship Afloat, a sixteen-ship battle royale inside a closing storm. Your progress is saved: unlock new ships, sails and paint as you play.
