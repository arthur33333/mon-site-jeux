GOKART RUSH

Kart Rush is a kart racing game with items. Pick up the question boxes and fire rockets, drop oil, throw bombs or call thunder on the karts in front of you. Drift in the corners for a turbo, use your nitro and try a rocket start when the lights go green. There are 12 stages, 4 cups, Time Trial with a ghost and an Eliminator mode. Win coins and buy faster karts in the garage.
