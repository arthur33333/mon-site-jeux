CANDY BUBBLE 2

Candy Bubble 2 is a fun bubble shooter with a cute fox, 40 levels and 5 candy worlds. Shoot candies, match three of the same color and make the rest fall. Beat the big boss at the end of each world, play the bonus stages for coins, use bombs and rainbow candies and fill the Sugar Rush bar for a fire shot. Endless mode is there for the best score.
