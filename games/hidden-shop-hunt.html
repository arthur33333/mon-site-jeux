Hidden Shop Hunt

Hidden Shop Hunt is an exciting hidden object puzzle game where you explore colorful shops and search for mystery items. Find the objects shown in the tiles around the shop scene. In some levels, click the question mark and solve a puzzle to reveal a mystery object, then find it in the shop before time runs out. Use hints if you get stuck and complete all 20 levels.
