Gravepowder

GRAVEPOWDER is a dark-fantasy third-person action game. The dead have risen over a frozen parish, and you are the last Warden: an axe in one hand, a flintlock in the other. Strike, roll, parry when the ring burns gold, then finish the staggered foe with a Grave Rite. Campaign: cross the frozen parish act by act. Kindle salt pyres to rest and travel, wear the armour the dead drop, buy from roadside traders, open Vaults and lay five colossal keepers to rest, among them the Ossuary Saint. Your journey is saved in the browser. Characters: choose one of five oaths - Headsman, Knight, Shade, Reaper or Warden - each with its own weapons, Rites and pace. Modes: play the story alone, or online co-op: light a Salt Beacon and a stranger can join your world (or answer someone elses). Gallows Circle: an online 4v4 brawl, bots fill empty seats. Plays with keyboard and mouse, or touch on phones held sideways.
