Save the Pets

Save the Pets is a funny drawing puzzle game. Angry bees are about to pour out of the hive, and a nervous shiba needs your help! HOW TO PLAY: draw one line to build a wall, a roof or a clever shape that shields the puppy. When you lift your finger the bees swarm out, and your drawing must protect the pup for 10 seconds. Your ink is limited, so think before you draw. LEVELS: 72 handmade levels. Each one adds new twists: water, spikes, bombs, ice, rocks, spiders, fans and balloons. Choose any unlocked level from the level map and replay to improve your score. STARS &amp; REWARDS: use less ink to earn up to 3 stars, collect coins and unlock new dog skins in the shop. HELPERS: stuck? Watch a short ad to see a Tips line, or skip a level. CHARACTERS: a cute shiba, adorable new dog skins and a crowd of grumpy bees. Physics, creativity and brain-teasing fun in one simple line. Your progress is saved automatically.
