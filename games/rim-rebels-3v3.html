Rim Rebels 3v3

Grab two teammates and take the blacktop. Rim Rebels 3v3 is full-court street basketball with two hoops, a running clock and four short quarters. Hang in the air before you release a jumper, fake a pass to open the lane, throw an alley-oop to a cutting teammate or swat a shot at the rim. Play the CPU on Easy, Normal or Hard, jump into Quick Match online, or create a room and send the code to a friend. Every quarter starts with a jump ball and each team gets one Super Shot per quarter.
