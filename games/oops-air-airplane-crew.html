Oops Air: Airplane Crew

Dear passengers, this is your captain speaking. We have a small problem in the cabin... and on the wing... and in the hold. Oops! You are the whole crew of the wobbliest airline in the sky. Load the bags, board the passengers, fill the tanks, take off, keep everyone alive in the air and land the plane in one piece. Probably. Fly from the cockpit, serve hungry passengers, calm the angry ones, bring a medkit to the sick. Tow baggage trolleys with the airport tug and drive the fuel truck to the wing. One GRAB button does it all: a suitcase, a passenger, a fire extinguisher. Everything goes wrong: turbulence, a bird in the engine and a fire on the wing, doors bursting open in the sky, jammed landing gear, icing. Weird cargo: a growing whale, an escaping crab, sharks and a real T-Rex in a cage. 12 routes: fields, the coast, the desert, night flights, mountains, a storm, a volcano, a dinosaur island. Dont want to fly alone? Take up to three helper bots.
