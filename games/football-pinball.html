Football Pinball

Football Pinball is a pinball table shaped like a football pitch, seen from your own end. Two flippers are all you get. The ball rolls down at you, and whatever drains between them is a goal for the other side &mdash; there is a net down there too, so you get to watch it happen. Kick it back up the table, past three midfielders and a keeper who tracks it along his own line, and put it in the goal at the far end. Ninety seconds, more goals wins, level is a draw.
