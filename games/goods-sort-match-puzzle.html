Goods Sort Match Puzzle

Goods Sort Match Puzzle is a colorful shelf-sorting game. Drag drinks, snacks, toys, and other goods between shelves, then line up three identical items to clear them. Each match opens space and reveals more products. Plan your moves, manage empty slots, and clear the board before the timer runs out. Enjoy varied shelf layouts and helpful boosters with mouse or touch controls. Progress is saved in the current browser.
