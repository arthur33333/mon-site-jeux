Scamper Karts

Scamper Karts is a 3D kart racer with a story. Scamper Garage has a leaking roof, one kart held together with tape and rent due on Friday. You are the new crew chief, with a fox driver who never stops talking. Win races, keep the doors open, and find out why the question-mark boxes have been so kind lately. Hold a drift through a corner and let go to boost; every drift fills your nitro. Carry two of 16 items, from soap bubbles and jelly mines to a hound rocket and a switcheroo. Features: - Story: 18 chapters in 3 acts, choices that matter and 3 endings - 17 tracks in 8 places, each with its own weather, music and trick: boost pads, soft sand, puddles, tumbleweeds, lava vents, ice, gusts and snow ramps - Item Race and Speed Race, three cups each - 24 karts and 13 animal drivers to unlock, upgrades and paints - Time Attack with ghosts, Drift Score and a Daily Track - Progress saved automatically
