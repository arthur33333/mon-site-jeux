Parcel Pulse Toy Factory

Parcel Pulse is a colorful conveyor-belt puzzle set in a busy toy factory. Boxes roll along branching belts, and you control the switches that decide where each one goes. Flip the arrows at the right moment and every box lands in the dock with the same color. The factory grows with you across 200 handcrafted levels. New boxes bring new rules: some must visit the stamp machine, some need the priority lane, cold boxes must avoid the stamp, and express boxes pay a bonus when they arrive fast. Use the Magnet, Speed and Freeze power-ups when the belts get busy, earn stars and coins, complete daily missions and unlock new avatars and frames.
