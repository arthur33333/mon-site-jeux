Lunar Sweepers: Moon Dust Simulator

Lunar Sweepers: Moon Dust Simulator is a relaxing 3D cleaning game set on the Moon. The lunar surface is buried under centuries of thick grey dust, and you are the newest janitor of the Lunar Surface Division. Sweep the dust, fill your capsules and launch them to the base. Sell the dust at the Dust Deposit for credits, then spend them in the shop on stronger, faster cleaning tools. Visit the Robot Station to get helper robots that sweep the surface with you. Start on the plain around the base and clean your way out zone by zone. Every cleaned area unlocks the next one, and a short tutorial shows you the basics. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can come back and keep cleaning any time. Features: - Calm, satisfying cleaning in first person - Tool upgrades and helper robots - Zones to unlock across the Moon - Automatic saving
