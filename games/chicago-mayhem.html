CHICAGO MAYHEM

Chicago Mayhem is a third-person shooter where every gunman in the city fights for himself. Bots fight bots, police shoot anyone with a gun, and you are caught in the middle. Start with a pistol and take guns from those you take down: SMGs, shotguns, rifles, machine guns and snipers. Every kill raises your wanted level, from patrol cars and roadblocks to a police helicopter. Rival gangs drive in and jump out shooting. Steal cars, blow them up and hunt the arms truck for its guns. Day turns to night and rain falls while the fight goes on. How high can your bounty go?
