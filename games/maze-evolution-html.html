Maze Evolution HTML

Maze Evolution HTML is a browser-based recreation of the classic Flash puzzle game Maze Evolution. Guide the star through 28 different mazes while avoiding moving obstacles, rotating barriers, cannons and hidden traps. Some levels change as you progress, so move carefully and adapt quickly. Featuring flawless hit detection. This is an unofficial fan-made preservation project. Not recommended on mobile devices as they have less compatibility, which may lead to game-breaking bugs.
