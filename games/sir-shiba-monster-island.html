Sir Shiba: Monster Island

Sir Shiba: Monster Island puts a small Shiba Inu knight at the center of an idle-arcade loop set across a chain of floating islands. Sir Shiba attacks nearby monsters automatically, so the core activity is guiding him around the map, collecting their treasure and selling it for coins to grow the island tile by tile. The world expands from a starting island through a mountain, an underground cave, and a snowy peak, each area introducing new monsters, new treasure types, and new hunters to hire. Order boards, daily gifts, quests, trophies, and a wardrobe of outfits and costumes layer longer-term goals on top of the main loop.
