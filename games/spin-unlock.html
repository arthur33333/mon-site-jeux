Spin Unlock

Spin Unlock is a calm logic puzzle about steel rings and padlocks. Turn each ring until every gap rests on a padlock, and every padlock takes exactly one gap. Each board has a single solution you can reach by reasoning alone: no timers, no game over. LEVELS: 40 levels in four chapters (First Locks, Bent Rings, Straight Through, Three Ways), each with a new ring shape, then the endless Master Vault. MODES: the level path and the Daily Vault, a new board every day with a streak to keep. CHARACTERS: Rocco, the raccoon locksmiths apprentice, shows you the rules as you play. ALSO: treasures hide behind safe doors in five vault rooms (Workshop, Old Bank, Museum, Royal Treasury, Master Vault), and every full treasure set unlocks a rare ring metal. Hints show the next ring you can work out. Undo, restart, marks for the rings you are sure about, and 9 ring finishes and 3 table looks to unlock. Your progress is saved.
