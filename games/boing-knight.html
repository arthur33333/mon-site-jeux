Boing Knight

Boing Knight is a bouncy arcade platformer! Your little knight never stops jumping on his pogo stick. Steer him up a giant beanstalk tower, slam down for Big Bounces and stomp monsters to climb even faster. But hurry: the green goo is rising behind you! Play Adventure mode with 15 levels and 3 stars each, battle giant bosses like the Thorn King and the huge Goo King, or climb forever in Endless mode through 8 colorful worlds. Chain stomps into combos, trigger Fever Mode, grab power-ups and catch the rare Golden Slime. Features: - 15 Adventure levels with 3 stars each - Endless mode with 8 worlds - 4 boss fights, including the giant Goo King
