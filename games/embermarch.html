Embermarch

Embermarch is a first-person dark-fantasy action RPG. The winter that never ends has raised the dead: lift the last Ember Banner, lead your warband of pikes, bows and wardens north, light the old beacons and free the frozen villages. Parry and dodge on a sixth sense that warns you of every blow, and cleave through barrow-kings and frost beasts. Heroes: Bulwark (shield and blade), Ranger (bow), Ashcaster (fire magic). Campaign: 3 frozen regions - the Bleak Pines, the Barrows and the Sleetshore - with quests, villages to save and a boss at the end of each. Modes: solo march; co-op for 2-4 players with a 6-letter code; Banner Clash PvP 2v2, 3v3 and 1v1 duels. Endgame: harder War Table tiers, weekly Rime Vaults, Ember Ranks. Camp: hire soldiers, armour them, reforge your gear - every piece you wear shows on your hero.
