Paint &amp; Escape

Paint &amp; Escape is a neon stealth-action game. Sneak past the guards, paint your graffiti masterpiece on the glowing spot, then escape before they catch you! Every level is a newly built house, school, museum or arcade, and the guards get smarter as you go. Watch out for security cameras, guard dogs and armored guards. Splat guards with paint bombs, taunt them with dances and collect spray cans for 3 stars. Unlock upgrades, outfits and hats, and come back for daily gifts, quests and trophies.
