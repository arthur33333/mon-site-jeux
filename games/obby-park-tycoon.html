Obby Park Tycoon

Build your own obby park and watch it come alive! Step on the buttons to build rides, food stalls and new obby sections over a big slime lake. Blocky guests buy tickets, queue up and race your course - they jump, slip, fall in the slime and try again - and every guest pays you. The more you build, the more they pay. Test every new section yourself to open it for guests: 24 levels climb from a sunny meadow through Candy Land and the Cloud Kingdom up into Outer Space, and a second world, LAVA LAND, waits behind a portal. Moving and falling platforms, spinning bars, trampolines, slippery jelly, conveyor belts, vanishing clouds and laser floors. Grab the giant coins at the summits, buy skins in the Skin Shop, upgrade ticket prices, guests and tips, and take a break in the FUN ZONE with Ball Toss and Bumper Cars.
