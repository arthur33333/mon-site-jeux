Eggcavation

There is an egg. It is very big. Your job: crack it open. Chip the giant egg plate by plate in first person, fill your bucket and sell the shards at the terminal. Spend the cash on bigger tools (pickaxe, sledgehammer, jackhammer, plasma drill, laser cutter), dynamite, drones and gear. But careful: hit the same spot too hard and the egg gets ANGRY. The payout climbs right up until it wakes up and throws you across the room! Dig through shell, membrane and gold-veined ore to the glowing core, then hatch it. 10 strange specimens, then endless bigger eggs. Boss fights, random events (gold rushes, power cuts, egg sneezes, eggquakes), hatchling pets, loot crates, trophies, daily missions, a 7-day gift and a Lucky Wheel. Progress saves automatically. 9 languages. Plays on desktop, phone and tablet.
