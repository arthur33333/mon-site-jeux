Pixel Shooter Puzzle

Pixel Shooter Puzzle is a colorful 3D puzzle game where every cannon choice counts. Choose cannons from the front of the queue to blast matching blocks from a rotating voxel model. Reveal the layers underneath, plan around limited slots, and clear 200 pixel puzzles. Rotate and zoom to inspect each model, and use optional boosts when you need a hand. Progress is saved in the current browser.
