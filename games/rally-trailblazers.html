RALLY TRAILBLAZERS

Rally Trailblazers is a fast off-road rally racing game. Race against 4 AI rivals across 40 stages in 5 worlds: Desert Dunes, Muddy Forest, Snow Pass, Red Canyon and Night Rally. Beat the clock: every checkpoint adds extra seconds, so drive fast and stay on the track! Slide through mud, keep control on ice, jump the ramps and dodge rocks and barrels. Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next stage and earn up to 3 stars per level. Collect coins and gems, claim your Daily Reward, and visit the Garage to paint your car and upgrade Top Speed, Acceleration, Handling and Boost. Can you become the rally champion?
