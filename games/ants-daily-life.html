Ants Daily Life

This is an ant-evolution simulation and management game in which players control a single ant within an anthill to dig tunnels, attack enemies to obtain food, and transport mud and food. The player must transport the eggs laid by the queen to various functional zones&mdash;such as the soldier ant zone, the worker ant zone and the transporter ant zone&mdash;where they hatch into the corresponding ant types. You are then responsible for leading the other ants to expand the nest, directing the worker ants to build additional tunnels to withstand natural disasters, and leading the soldier ants to fend off invading enemies.
