Night Lake

Night Lake is a calm night fishing game with a touch of mystery. You are a lone fisherman in a small wooden boat, rowing by lantern light across dark water. Cast your float, wait for the bite and strike at the right moment, then fight the fish with your reel and rod while keeping the line tension safe. Sell your catch at the dock to earn coins and experience, then buy better baits, rods, lines, reels, keepnets and charms. Take on three quests every night and fill your field guide.
