Forest Brawl

Forest Brawl is a fast-paced single-player 3D voxel action game. Jump straight into Endless Survival mode and battle increasingly difficult waves of forest enemies, including Thorn Cutters, Moss Raiders, Spore Scouts, and the mighty Forest Warden boss. Use sword attacks, heavy strikes, dashes, parries, and Blade Time to build AWESOME combos and high scores. Choose upgrades between waves to improve your abilities. Fight across themed forest areas with destructible trees, rocks, and structures, dynamic day and night, moving grass, and spectacular voxel effects. How long can you survive?
