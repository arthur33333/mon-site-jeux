Flying Ants Quest

An open-world 3D ant adventure for one player. Carry crumbs home to the anthill, one at a time, before the clock runs out. Sniff out the next crumb, sprint, or spread your wings and fly. Beetles, spiders, wasps, a centipede and antlion pits stand in your way: bite back, or fly over them. Win a round, and new foes move in: small rival ants, then big ones, then praying mantises. Four rounds, from 6 crumbs in 7 minutes to 15 in 28, in a garden drawn like an ink sketch. Code by Claude through a custom build harness; design and tuning by Reuven Naor; CC0 sounds from BigSoundBank and Kenney. Six languages. Free.
