Blockcraft Racing Championship

Blockcraft Racing Championship is a blocky kart racer by BREYMANTECH. Race 7 rivals across 8 voxel tracks: Blocky Meadows, Sandstone Canyon, Frostbite Pass, Jungle Temple, Pixel Pier, Neon Block City, Lava Core and Sky Kingdom. Grab ? boxes for 11 power-ups like Homing Rockets, Block Bombs, TNT Traps, Freeze Orbs, Thunder and Diamond Power. Drift through corners to charge mini-turbos and smash crates, trees and barrels into flying blocks. Crash hard and your kart breaks apart piece by piece! Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next track, earn coins, unlock 6 karts and upgrade speed, acceleration and handling.
