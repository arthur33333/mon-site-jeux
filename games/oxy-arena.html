Oxy Arena

Oxy Arena is a fast online FPS in a blocky 3D world. Press Play to jump into a match with players from around the world, or create a private room and invite friends with a code. Modes: Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch (4 v 4), and Backrooms: Zombies, a solo survival mode with five waves of zombies in the endless yellow rooms. Practice against bots or on the target range. Maps: Downtown (a city with walk-in shops), Ruins, Orbital (a space station), Dungeon, Block Fort and the Backrooms. Guns: AK, SMG, shotgun, sniper and pistol. Chain headshots and multi-kills, level up, unlock gun skins and complete daily challenges. Plays on computers, phones
