Tidewash

Tidewash is a calm puzzle with a twist: the beach shrinks. Drag pieces onto the dry sand and build groups of three or more of the same colour. Every few placements a wave rolls in, one row higher. Groups that touch the water are washed away for points, lonely pieces turn into driftwood, and a big wash pushes the tide back. No timers and no lives: just you, the sand and the sea. Three worlds: - Tide: 28 hand-made levels that teach the game one idea at a time. - Storm 8x8: the big beach, with faster tides, more colours and bigger targets. - Magnet: pull lanes with colour magnets and collect every star chip in as few moves as you can. Every level has three stars to earn: reach the target, keep the beach clean and finish efficiently. Every Tide and Magnet level is checked by a solver, so if it looks impossible, it isnt. One free hint per level when you are stuck. Progress is saved in your browser.
