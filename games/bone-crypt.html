Bone Crypt

Bone Crypt is a 3D arena brawler in a haunted graveyard. Fight wave after wave of skeletons with sword combos, dodge rolls and a whirlwind ultimate. The dead dont stay down: every skeleton you beat collapses into a bone pile that rises again unless you smash it in time. After two rises, the next kill shatters it for good. Heroes: the Knight, and the Barbarian, who unlocks for 400 gold. Enemies: fast Minions, Warriors with axe and shield, Rogues with twin blades, and Mages that shoot bolts and raise bone piles from afar. Every fifth wave the giant Bone King storms in, slams the ground and calls minions.
