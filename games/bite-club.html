BITE CLUB

BITE CLUB is a late-night TV eating show gone wrong: a whole set is flooded with giant dessert and you have to eat your way out in first person. Every bite carves a real tunnel through jelly, sponge cake, chocolate and candy. When your belly is full, waddle back to the WEIGH-IN scale and get paid by the kilo. Keep chewing to build HYPE, buy bigger bellies, a turbo jaw and better utensils, dig out hidden rooms, collect 24 studio props and escape all 6 TV sets.
