Roll Rush: Ice Cream Tycoon

Roll Rush: Ice Cream Tycoon is a cozy 3D tycoon game where you run your own rolled ice cream stand on a sunny beach! GAMEPLAY: Scrape creamy ice cream rolls off the frozen plate, stock your display and sell to customers at the till. Customers bargain, so check what each order is worth before you accept! LEVELS &amp; UPGRADES: Unlock 16 flavors, rare toppings, bigger plots, better tools and a crew that works for you. Build a Milkshake Blender and Sandwich Press, and grow your stand into a cafe, a resort and a giant Ice Cream Palace. MODES &amp; EVENTS: Crowd Rushes, Golden Events, Beach Parties and Rush Hour. Rebirth to unlock new topping seasons, VIPs, celebrities and royals! CHARACTERS: Play as a cheerful ice cream chef, serve dozens of beachgoers and hire workers to help run the shop
