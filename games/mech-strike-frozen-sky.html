Mech Strike: Frozen Sky

Mech Strike: Frozen Sky is a fast third-person mech flight shooter. Your battle mech has wings, jet thrusters, twin cannons and shoulder missiles: launch over a shattered frozen sea at sunset, race between glacier walls and shoot enemy mechs out of the sky. SURVIVAL RUN: scouts, hunter packs and named aces attack wave after wave. Clear a wave, take a run boost (firepower, rapid fire, plating, repair, afterburner, missile rack). Four frames - lose them all and the run is over. Beat your best wave. CAMPAIGN: 18 flown missions in three theatres - raid bases, break relay towers under flak and bring down giant Colossus bosses with your wingmen. MULTIPLAYER: play co-op with friends using a room code, or 6v6 online air battles (bots fill empty seats). BUILD YOUR MECH: earn gold for wings, armour, plasma cannons and missile salvos - every upgrade changes how your mech looks. Six mech frames with signature skills.
