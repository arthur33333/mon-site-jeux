Kingmoon

Kingmoon is a 3D space action game. King Regulus rules the stars from his moon, and his robot and alien kingdoms hold every planet. You start with one small ship and one flag. Fly low over five worlds, each with its own rules: green Veyra, the ice of Glacia, the volcanoes of Pyra, the sandstorms of Sair and the machine planet Nucleon. Land, fight on foot and plant your flag to turn each sector to your side. Call your mech when the fight gets heavy. Modes and levels: 25 story missions in 5 systems, each system ending with a boss, plus the final war on the Kings Moon. Characters: four pilots, Lira and the robot commander Vexar. Earn crystals, upgrade and paint your ship in the hangar. Progress is saved automatically. 6 languages.
