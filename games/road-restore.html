Road Restore

Road Restore is a 3D game about fixing roads, one stretch at a time. Drive your truck over damaged asphalt, repaint road markings, and install guardrails across 70 sectors, from quiet forests to coastal roads and mountain passes. Your tools work automatically as you drive. When supplies run low, head back to the depot for a free refill. Use the money you earn to improve your truck, unlock better equipment, and pick up new skins along the way. Move on when the next sector opens, or stay a little longer to finish every job and earn all three stars. You can return to earlier sectors with your upgraded truck or take on repeatable contracts.
