Storky Number Drop

Storky Number Drop is a juicy merge puzzle where every number is a wobbly bag of water! Pick one of three bags, pull Storkys rubber band back and let go. Touch two bags with the same number and they melt together into one bag with double the number: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, and on and on. The bags are real liquid: they squash, stretch and flow into every gap, but they never overlap. Easy to learn, hard to put down. How big can you go?
