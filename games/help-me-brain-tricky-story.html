Help Me: Brain Tricky Story

Help Me: Brain Tricky Story is a funny brain teaser full of silly cartoon stories. The stick-figure characters keep getting into awkward little troubles, and only your clever thinking can get them out! LEVELS: 31 short, hand-drawn levels, each with its own crazy situation. Pick caterpillars out of a lunch box with chopsticks, help a poor dog stuck with a sea urchin, and much more. Some puzzles use physics and flowing water. HOW TO PLAY: tap the scene to take a closer look, then find the trick. Grab and drag objects to fix the problem. Every level hides a different idea, so think outside the box! MODES: play the stories in order, or replay any unlocked level from the level menu. HELPERS: stuck? Watch a short ad to get a Hint, or to Skip the level. CHARACTERS: goofy stick-figure heroes, a grumpy sheep, a sad dog, an angry bee and other funny friends. Your progress is saved automatically. Language: English.
