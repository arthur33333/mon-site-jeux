Hungry Snail

**Hungry Snail** is a fun logic puzzle game where you help a cute snail find its way home. Move ground tiles to create the perfect path, avoid tricky obstacles, and collect as many stars as possible along the way. Each level introduces more challenging puzzles that test your logic, strategy, and problem-solving skills. Plan every move, collect all the stars, and guide the hungry snail safely home!
