Clearfell: Forest Outpost

Clearfell: Forest Outpost is an isometric 3D base-building survival game. Start with one tent in a small clearing deep in an endless forest. Chop your way out, gather wood, stone, gold and iron, and build sawmills, mines, walls, turrets and mortars. The forest hides goblin camps, caged prisoners, ore veins and wandering merchants. Rescue settlers and put them to work. Every other night goblins raid your base along their trails. Fight back with guns and grenades, then follow the trails and burn their lairs - but new ones rise deeper in the woods. Day and night, weather, research, trophies and achievements. English and Russian.
