Teleport Blade: Ninja Strike

Throw your blade and teleport to it! Teleport Blade: Ninja Strike is a fast one-touch action game. Aim, throw your weapon, and your ninja instantly teleports to wherever it lands. Time slows down while you aim, so line up the perfect strike and take out every enemy before they take you down. LEVELS: 25 handcrafted stages that keep looping, so the action never ends. Every 5th level is a boss fight: hit the boss several times to win. ENEMIES: blocky voxel foes that fight back. Some patrol platforms, some hide behind shields, others shoot guns, bazookas and cannons at you. Smash through breakable walls and avoid bombs. CHARACTERS &amp; SHOP: earn coins for every enemy you defeat and spend them on new ninja outfits and weapons. MODE: single-player level mode with short, replayable stages that are easy to learn and hard to master. Works on desktop and mobile. Your level, coins and unlocked items are saved automatically.
